=== PAL - palettes ===

Nothing here exists yet - there is no palette keyword in BASIC816 at all,
so this page is entirely a plan. SETCOLOR is the one statement that
would sit on top of it; it is reserved and refuses.

[ ] PAL index,rgb - set palette entry 0-255 to a 12-bit $RGB colour.
    PAL 1,$0F00 makes colour 1 pure red.
[ ] PALGET index - read an entry back. See the warning below.
[ ] PALLOAD file$,start - load entries from a file, starting at an index.
[ ] PALSAVE file$,start,count - save a RANGE, not all 256. See below.
[ ] SETCOLOR index,rgb - the BASIC816 spelling, for the 16 text colours.

VERA has 256 entries, each 12-bit ($0RGB: four bits per channel). Entry 0
is the background/transparent colour, and entries 0-15 default to the
C64/X16 sixteen.

VERA2 has a palette of its own, written through a different path: an
index register at $9F66 that auto-increments, then low and high halves at
$9F67/$9F68 where writing the high half commits the entry. Two palettes,
two mechanisms - a PAL statement will have to say which layer it means.

Do not trust a palette readback. Entries your program wrote come back
exactly; entries nobody ever wrote come back as something that is not the
palette in use. That is why a save takes a range rather than assuming
256 - save the entries you set. Better still, keep the palette in an
array in your program and save THAT, and nothing depends on a readback
at all.
