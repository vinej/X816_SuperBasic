=== VERAFX - the accelerators ===

Two separate pieces of hardware sit under this page, and neither is
reachable from SuperBasic yet.

VERA FX adds a 16x16 multiplier, a 32-bit write cache for fast VRAM
fills, transparent writes, and address helpers for lines, polygons and
affine sampling. Its registers are at $9F29-$9F2C, banked behind DCSEL.

[ ] DCSEL n - select the register bank 0-63 so the FX registers can be
    reached at all.
[ ] FXMULT(a,b) - signed 16x16 to 32-bit multiply, in hardware. Worth a
    keyword of its own: SuperBasic's integer multiply is a software
    shift-add loop (X816/ints_x816.s) and this is far faster.
[ ] FXFILL byte,bank,addr,count - fill VRAM through the write cache,
    roughly four times a plain loop. A count that is not a multiple of
    four is fine; the leftover bytes go out plain.
[ ] FXCLEAR bank,addr,count - zero a region.
[ ] FXOFF - put the helpers back so ordinary VRAM writes work. Forgetting
    this is the classic way to corrupt the next thing you draw.

BLIT816 is the core's own blitter - see X816_core/doc/BLIT816.md. It is
not VERA FX and it is not on the X16; it moves rectangles around SDRAM,
which is where the VERA2 framebuffer lives (HELP GRAPHIC).

[ ] BLIT sx,sy,dx,dy,w,h - copy a rectangle.
[ ] BLITMASK - the same with a transparent colour, which is what makes
    it useful for drawing characters over a background.

For a BASIC these two matter more than they would for assembly. An
interpreted per-pixel loop is hopeless for anything full-screen, so any
statement that fills, scrolls or draws a shape should end up calling one
of these rather than looping - the difference is the whole gap between a
usable graphics BASIC and a slideshow.
