=== AUDIOYM - raw YM2151 registers ===

The YM2151 (IKAOPM in the core) sits at $9F40 address / $9F41 data, in
the I/O page at $00:9F00. Its 256 registers are the ones in the YM2151
datasheet: $08 KEYON, $20+ per channel, $60+ total level, and so on.

SuperBasic has no audio keywords at all today. Everything here is a plan.

[x] YMPOKE reg,value - write a YM2151 register. The escape hatch: every
    FM statement on the AUDIOFM page is shorthand for some of these.
[ ] YMPEEK(reg) - what was last written there. A shadow, not a probe:
    the chip answers writes only, there is no register readback in the
    hardware, so a program that wants to know must remember.

Until a statement exists you can drive the chip with POKE, and this is a
perfectly reasonable thing to do from BASIC:

    POKE $9F40,8    : REM address register = $08 (KEYON)
    POKE $9F41,$78  : REM data

The one rule that matters: the chip has a BUSY flag and you must wait for
it to clear before writing the address and again before the data. A
write that goes out early is silently wrong. Any wait must be BOUNDED -
a chip that never cleared busy would otherwise hang the machine, and an
early write is the better failure.

The YM can also raise an interrupt, KIRQ_YM (slot 6 in the kernel's IRQ
table), for its timers.

For notes, envelopes and instruments rather than registers, HELP AUDIOFM.
